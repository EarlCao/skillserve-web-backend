SkillServe Terms of Service

These terms apply to everyone who uses the SkillServe mobile app as a customer or as a service provider. By creating an account you agree to them. SkillServe administrators may update these terms; the version shown in the app is the one in force.

1. What SkillServe is
SkillServe is a marketplace that connects customers in the Philippines with independent service providers. A booking is an agreement between the customer and the provider. SkillServe provides the app, verification, messaging, reviews, reports and dispute handling, but is not a party to the service itself and does not employ providers.

2. Who can use SkillServe
You must be at least 18 years old. You must give your real name, birthday and contact details, and keep them up to date. One person may hold one customer account and one provider account. You are responsible for everything done with your account, so keep your password private.

3. Identity verification
To book or offer services you may be asked to verify your identity with your Philippine National ID (PhilSys card or ePhilID). The photos must be clear, unedited pictures of your own card. A National ID can verify only one customer account and one provider account. Providers may also be asked for certificates or other documents before their profile is shown to customers. Submitting someone else's ID or altered documents leads to the account being banned.

4. Bookings and cancellations
The price shown for a service is the full price the customer pays. A provider confirms a booking before it is scheduled. Either side may cancel within the rules shown in the app; a late cancellation may carry the fee shown before you confirm it. Do not arrange to take a booking outside the app to avoid these rules.

5. Payment
Customers pay the provider directly, by GCash to the provider's own account or in cash on the day. SkillServe never receives or holds the customer's money, so it cannot refund or reverse a payment; problems with a booking are handled through a dispute (section 8). Providers record when they have been paid.

6. SkillServe's commission
SkillServe's commission is included in the provider's price, not added on top. After a booking is paid, the provider owes SkillServe its commission and pays it in the app. While a commission stays unpaid past the limits set by SkillServe, the provider cannot accept new bookings.

7. Messages, reviews and photos
Use chat only for the booking it belongs to. Reviews must describe a real, completed booking honestly. Anything you post must follow the Community Guidelines. You keep ownership of what you upload and allow SkillServe to store and show it as needed to run the service.

8. Reports and disputes
You can report a person, a review, a message, a service or a provider, and raise a dispute on a booking that is in progress or completed, with photos as proof. SkillServe administrators review every report and dispute and may contact both parties. Their decision on the platform side (for example a warning, removed content, or a suspended account) is final within SkillServe; it does not replace your rights under Philippine law.

9. Suspension and removal
SkillServe may warn, suspend or ban an account, hide a service, or remove content that breaks these terms or the Community Guidelines, including fraud, harassment, unsafe conduct, fake reviews or repeated no-shows.

10. Closing your account
Accounts cannot be deleted from the app, so that money owed between users — a booking, a payment to return — can always be traced. Contact SkillServe support if you need help with your account. Some records (such as completed bookings and moderation decisions) are kept as long as the law or a dispute requires.

If a booking you were paid for (in full or as a downpayment) is cancelled, you must return that payment to the customer within the time SkillServe sets and upload proof. A provider who does not may be blocked from new bookings, suspended or banned. A customer who cancels late may let the provider keep the late-cancellation fee shown before cancelling.

11. Liability
SkillServe works to keep the marketplace safe but cannot guarantee the quality, timing or safety of a provider's work or a customer's conduct. To the extent the law allows, SkillServe is not liable for losses arising from a service agreed between a customer and a provider.
