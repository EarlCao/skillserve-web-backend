SkillServe Community Guidelines

SkillServe works when customers and providers can trust each other. These guidelines apply to profiles, services, chat, reviews, photos, reports and support requests.

Be honest
- Use your real name and your own National ID. One person, one account.
- Describe services, prices, experience and qualifications truthfully. Upload only photos of your own work.
- Leave reviews only for bookings that really happened, and describe them fairly.

Be respectful
- No harassment, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory or sexual comments.
- Keep chat about the booking. Do not send unwanted messages after a booking is over.
- Respect the other person's home, property and privacy. Do not share their details or photos outside the app.

Be safe
- Do not offer, request or carry out anything illegal or dangerous.
- Providers: follow the safety practices your trade requires and turn down work you are not qualified for.
- If you ever feel unsafe, leave and contact the authorities first, then report it in the app.

Keep it on SkillServe
- Book, message and record payments through the app, so both sides are protected by reports and disputes.
- Do not ask to be paid outside the agreed booking to avoid SkillServe's rules or commission.
- Show up on time. If plans change, cancel or reschedule in the app as early as you can.

Photos and proof
- Photos you send in chat, reports, disputes or support tickets must be real, unedited and relevant. Do not include other people's faces or ID numbers unless it is necessary to show the problem.
- National ID photos must be clear pictures of your own card, with no filters or edits.

Reporting problems
Use Report on a person, message, review, service or provider, or raise a dispute on a booking, and add photos if they help. SkillServe administrators review every report. False or malicious reports break these guidelines too.

What happens if the guidelines are broken
Depending on how serious it is, SkillServe may remove content, hide a service, warn the account, suspend it for a time, or ban it permanently.
