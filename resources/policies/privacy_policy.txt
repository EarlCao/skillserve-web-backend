SkillServe Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data SkillServe collects, why, and the choices you have. SkillServe processes personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. What we collect
- Account details: your name, email address, mobile number, birthday, address (region, province, city and barangay) and profile photo.
- Identity verification: your National ID card number, name and birthday as printed on the card, and photos of the front and back of the card. The card number is stored only as a protected, one-way code plus its last four digits.
- Provider details: business name, services, prices, service areas, availability, portfolio photos, GCash name and number, and any certificates you submit.
- Activity: bookings, payments you record, commissions, messages and photos sent in chat, reviews, reports, disputes, support tickets and the photos attached to them, and notifications.
- Technical data: sign-in times and the device information needed to keep you signed in and send notifications.

2. Why we use it
To create and secure your account; to verify that you are who you say you are and that one ID backs one account; to show providers and services and handle bookings, chat and reviews; to calculate and collect SkillServe's commission; to review reports and disputes; to answer support requests; to send codes, booking updates and announcements you have not switched off; and to meet legal obligations.

3. Who can see it
- Other users see what the app shows them: a provider's public profile, services and reviews; the name of the other person on your booking and the messages you exchange.
- SkillServe administrators see what they need for their role, such as verification documents, reports, disputes and support tickets. Their access is logged.
- Service providers that run SkillServe for us: hosting and database (Render, Neon), email delivery (Brevo), commission payments (PayMongo) and Google sign-in when you choose it. They process data only to provide their service to SkillServe.
- Reading your ID card in the app happens on your phone; only the photos you submit are uploaded.
We do not sell personal data.

4. How long we keep it
- Photos of a National ID that was not accepted are deleted automatically after a set period (90 days unless SkillServe sets otherwise). Photos of a verified National ID are kept while your account exists, so SkillServe can act on fraud or scam reports. The protected card-number code is kept so an ID cannot back a second account.
- Commission receipt screenshots are deleted after a set period (180 days unless SkillServe sets otherwise).
- Accounts cannot be deleted from the app, so payments owed between users can always be traced; contact SkillServe support for help with your account. Booking, payment and moderation records may be kept longer where the law or an open dispute requires.

5. How we protect it
Data travels over encrypted connections. Verification documents, chat photos, and photos attached to reports, disputes and support tickets are stored privately and are only shown to the people allowed to see them after they sign in.

6. Your rights
You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, or receive a copy of it. Most details can be changed in Profile; for anything else, contact us through Help & Support. If you believe your data has been mishandled, you may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

7. Changes
SkillServe may update this policy. The version shown in the app is the one in force.
